# E.5: web UI accounts, channels and layout

Do not start this spec until every rule in E.1 to E.4 passes.

## Tips

- **Never use `alert()`, `confirm()` or `prompt()`.** Show errors and choices in the page.

## Uses

| Function | Used here for |
|---|---|
| The auth, workspace and channel routes | Everything on this page |

## Adds

These `data-testid` values and form field names are also fixed:

| Element | Fixed name |
|---|---|
| Fields in `auth-form` | `name="username"`, `name="password"` |
| Fields in `workspace-create-form` | `name="slug"`, `name="name"`, and a `type="submit"` button |
| Field in `create-channel-form` | `name="name"` |
| Sign-up or login error | `auth-error` |
| Channel creation error | `create-channel-error` |

## Rules

The rules run in a browser at 1280x900. Elements must be really visible when they are used.

5.1 **Sign-up error.** The page opens on a login form in `auth-form`, and `auth-toggle` switches it to
    sign-up. While logged out, `logout-btn` and `message-input` are not visible. Signing up with the password `x` shows a non-empty `auth-error` and logs nobody in.

5.2 **Sign-up logs in.** A valid sign-up hides `auth-form`, stores the token in localStorage under
    `huddle.token`, and shows the username in `current-user`.

5.3 **Workspace.** With no workspace yet, `empty-state-create-workspace` is visible. Submitting
    `workspace-create-form` hides it and shows the workspace's name in `workspace-header`.

5.4 **Logout and login.** `logout-btn` removes `huddle.token` and shows `auth-form` as a login form. Logging
    in with the same username and password shows the same workspace in `workspace-header`.

5.5 **Channels.** `new-channel-btn` opens `create-channel-form`. Submitting it with
    `create-channel-submit` adds a `channel-entry` to `channel-list`, whose clickable element has
    `data-channel-id` and `data-channel-name` and whose text starts with `#`. Clicking an entry
    switches to that channel: `channel-title` shows its name and `channel-topic` its topic.

5.6 **Duplicate channel name.** Creating a channel with a name already used shows a non-empty
    `create-channel-error` and adds no entry.

5.7 **Empty channel.** A channel with no messages shows non-blank text in `message-list`.

5.8 **Layout.** `channel-list` and `workspace-header` are in a sidebar on the left with a coloured
    background (not white or transparent), and `message-list` is to its right. `thread-panel` is not
    visible until a thread is opened, and `channel-settings-close` and `workspace-settings-close` are
    not visible until their dialogs are opened. `message-input` sits at the bottom of the window, with
    its lower edge within 120 pixels of the bottom.
